AE06 — Frontier AI Lab

Research is an engineering skill even if you do not plan to publish. Half the module is seminars on emerging AI topics. The other half: experiment design, practical work, and presentations.

Tư vấn học phần này Lịch khai giảng

Prerequisites

AE03 and AE04 completed. AE05 helps if you bring a system into the experiments.

Outcomes

Research report and prototype, with an optional manuscript or contribution package.

Audience

AI engineers, technical leads, and builders who want research skills, with an optional paper track.

Core capability

Conduct applied research on emerging AI topics selected each year.

Syllabus

16 sessions in 8 weeks.

WeeksFocus AFocus B
1–2Seminars on this year's frontier: read papers, extract claims, ask questionsResearch skills: question, hypothesis, and experiment scope
3–4Design experiments: baseline, metrics, and success criteriaReproduce or test a claim; log results and limitations
5–6Prototype / ablation on the sandbox and allocated GPUsResearch clinic: mentor review of direction and evidence
7–8Write the research report and present resultsDefense: explain, revise live, and decide the next step
Lab

Tools & access

Research sandbox, API budget, GPUs when needed, research clinics, and mentor review.

CV

Evidence you keep

Research report, prototype, and optional paper or contribution package.
